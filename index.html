Craft

What goes into the work.

Brand Identity+

It starts with an interview. I have you envision the mark, ask what clean looks like to you, and mock it up fast with AI. The rule in the AI age is that nothing can look AI. People can spot it and they do not want it. So I use every tool available, Photoshop, Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT, Midjourney, then refine until the AI fingerprints are gone and it just looks like your brand.

Event Graphics+

An event mark has one job: make people feel like they are part of it. I pull references from art I love, because combining multiple ideas into one is where the good stuff happens, and the palette has to match the vibe of the event. Then I get extra eyes on it before it ships. Everyone sees something different, and that catches what I cannot.

Podcast & Media Art+

The whole game is restraint. The first cut of Fuming Takes did too much and you could see the AI in it. What works is a simple design you can read in a second and fully understand, because that is all the time a thumbnail gets.

Apparel & Print+

Shirt work is its own discipline. Every color has to mesh with the fabric, which can mean recoloring text and borders so the design stays distinct from the shirt itself. It cuts both ways though. Pick the right shirt color and it becomes part of the design. Several pieces here are on shirts right now, specs and all.

AI Generation, Directed+

No single AI tool is perfect, so I use all of them where they are strongest. I interview the client to get the vision, then have Claude interview me to sharpen the direction. We run revisions until the concept is right, then an advanced prompt goes into an image generator for the starting point, and Photoshop takes it home.

Photoshop & Traditional Tools+

The last five percent is always by hand. Distorting, recoloring, moving pieces around, and cleaning out the little AI blemishes that AI itself cannot edit out. That is the difference between generated and professional, and it is the same Photoshop that built the original Finnegan Hauling badge and the Behind The Venue cover from scratch.

Frequently Asked

Quick answers.

Do you take client projects?+

Yes. Most of the work here started as a business need, a cause worth helping, or a friend with an idea. Send a note with the idea and a rough timeline.

What tools do you work in?+

Photoshop plus every AI tool worth using: Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT, Midjourney. No single one is perfect, so the tool depends on the job. The one rule is that the finished work cannot look AI.

What does AI Generation with Creative Direction mean?+

I interview the client to get the vision, have Claude interview me to sharpen it, and run revisions until the concept is right. Then an advanced prompt goes into an image generator for a starting point and Photoshop takes it the rest of the way. The tag on each project marks which pieces used that pipeline and which were built entirely by hand.

What else do you do?+

Frontier AI evaluation, building Limitless, and co-owning Finnegan Hauling. The rest of the resume lives on LinkedIn. This page is for the design work.
